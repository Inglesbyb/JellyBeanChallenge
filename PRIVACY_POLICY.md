# Privacy Policy for Jelly Bean: 60s Challenge

**Last Updated & Effective Date:** November 2, 2026  
**Application Name:** Jelly Bean: 60s Challenge  
**Package Identifier:** `com.jellybean.challenge`  

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Jelly Bean: 60s Challenge** ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application on Android and web platforms.

**Our Core Privacy Promise:**  
We believe gaming should be fun, fast, and private. **Jelly Bean: 60s Challenge does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.** You can play all 20 mini-games, complete daily gauntlets, and customize your mascot without creating an account or providing any private data.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any of the following:
- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or usernames.
- **No Account Registration:** The game uses a guest profile system generated entirely on your device.
- **No Location Data:** We do not access or collect GPS, coarse location, or cell tower location data.
- **No Device Identifiers for Tracking:** We do not track or store your Advertising ID (GAID), IMEI, MAC address, or serial numbers.
- **No Financial Information:** We do not collect credit card numbers, billing information, or bank details.
- **No Sensor or Media Access:** We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, or photos.

---

## 3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide game functionality, save your progress, and remember your preferences, the Game stores the following data **locally on your device** (via device storage and browser `localStorage`):

1. **Guest Profile & Callsign:** The nickname you set (e.g. `Captain Bean`) and selected emoji avatar.
2. **Gameplay Progression & Statistics:** Lifetime points, cycles survived, individual mini-game high scores, and stages cleared.
3. **Mascot Closet Unlocks:** Equipped skins and hats unlocked through gameplay milestones.
4. **Daily Seed Progress:** Current day seed completion record, daily medal tier (Gold, Silver, Bronze), and active daily streak.
5. **Game Preferences:** SFX sound volume slider, haptic vibration toggle, and screen shake toggle.

**Important:** This data resides exclusively in your device's sandboxed local storage. It is never uploaded to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party networks.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

- **No Third-Party Advertising:** The Game does not include third-party ad banners, interstitials, or tracking SDKs.
- **No Analytics Tracking:** We do not use third-party user behavioral tracking services (such as Facebook SDK, AppsFlyer, or Adjust).
- **Native Hardware APIs:** We use standard open-source Capacitor runtime bridges (`@capacitor/app`, `@capacitor/haptics`, `@capacitor/status-bar`) strictly to communicate with your device's local vibration hardware and system navigation buttons. These libraries do not collect or transmit telemetry.

## 5. Sharing Feature (Web Share API)

When you choose to use the optional "📸 SHARE RUN POSTER" or "📸 SHARE DAILY RECORD" feature:
- The poster image is rendered dynamically on your device's canvas.
- If you tap "Share", the native operating system share dialog (`navigator.share`) opens, allowing **you** to select where to send the image (such as messaging apps or social networks).
- We have no access to your recipient list, message text, or shared files. If you do not tap the share button, no share action occurs.

- We comply fully with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K)**.
- We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
- Because no personal data is collected from any user, parents can be confident that their children's privacy is preserved.

## 7. How to Delete Your Data

Because all game data is stored locally on your device, you have complete and immediate control over it at all times:

1. **In-App Reset:** In the main menu, click **"Start fresh run"** to reset current session progress.
2. **Complete Data Deletion (Android):**
   - Open your Android device **Settings**.
   - Navigate to **Apps & notifications** (or **App Management**) > **Jelly Bean: 60s Challenge**.
   - Tap **Storage & cache**.
   - Tap **Clear Storage** (or **Clear Data**).  
   *This immediately wipes all local profile data, unlocked skins, and scores permanently.*
3. **Uninstalling the App:** Uninstalling the app from your device automatically deletes all associated local data.

## 8. Security

Because we do not transmit or store personal information on servers, there is no risk of remote server breaches compromising your personal data. Local data is protected by Android's application sandboxing architecture.
