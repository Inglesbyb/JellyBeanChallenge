Privacy Policy

🎮 Application: Jelly Bean: 60s Challenge 📦 Package: com.jellybean.challenge 📅 Updated: November 2, 2026
🔒 Our Core Privacy Promise

Jelly Bean: 60s Challenge does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. All gameplay scores, mascot customizations, and daily streaks are stored strictly on your local device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in pure arcade fun without invasive tracking. We do not collect:

  • No Personal Identifiers: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • No Account Sign-In: No registration required; guests can play immediately.
  • No Location Data: Zero GPS, coarse, or cell tower location tracking.
  • No Advertising Identifiers: We do not read or store your Google Advertising ID (GAID).
  • No Financial Info: No payment details or billing data.
  • No Hardware Access: No camera, microphone, contact list, or photo library permissions.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To preserve your arcade achievements across play sessions, the following is stored locally inside your device's sandboxed storage:

  • Callsign & Avatar: Your chosen player nickname and mascot icon.
  • Progression & Stats: High scores, cycles survived, and stage clears.
  • Mascot Closet: Equipped skins (Cyan, Berry, Gold, etc.) and hats unlocked via points.
  • Daily Seed Gauntlet: Today's completion status, medal grade (🥇/🥈/🥉), and daily streak count.
  • Settings: Audio volume, haptic feedback toggle, and screen shake toggle.

This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The game contains no third-party ad networks (no banner ads, no popups) and no third-party behavioral analytics SDKs. Open-source Capacitor plugins are used exclusively to bridge native device hardware (vibration actuator and back button navigation).

4. Optional Run Card Sharing

When you choose to tap the "📸 SHARE RUN POSTER" or "📸 SHARE DAILY RECORD" button, the poster image is drawn directly on your device canvas. Tapping "Share" triggers the native Android system share dialog (navigator.share), allowing you to select an app of your choice. We have no access to your recipients or external accounts.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

The game is family-friendly and designed for all ages. Because we collect zero personal data from any user, we are fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and European GDPR-K regulations.

6. How to Delete Your Data

You can permanently erase all local game data at any time:

  • In-Game: On the main menu, click "Start fresh run" to clear session data.
  • Android Settings: Go to Settings > Apps > Jelly Bean: 60s Challenge > Storage > Clear Storage.
  • Uninstall: Removing the app from your device immediately deletes all local save files.
